● ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Organizing Research for Better Discovery

KNOWLEDGEVAULT is a centralized research knowledge management system that connects researchers, research papers, publication venues, research areas, projects, keywords, citations and paper reviews. It uses a relational database to organize academic information and provide efficient research discovery.
